# Sample Loan Approval Policy

This is illustrative test data, not a real lending policy.

## Application intake
The applicant submits identity, income, employment and requested amount details. Loan Operations checks mandatory fields. If information is missing, the application remains pending and the applicant is contacted. When information arrives, completeness is checked again.

## Verification
The Verification Team validates identity documents and consistency. Invalid or inconsistent documents trigger a clarification request and another verification attempt. An unresolved material discrepancy results in decline.

## Credit and risk assessment
The Credit Team obtains a credit report and reviews repayment history, obligations and income. If the report is unavailable, the application is put on hold. Applications failing the lender's eligibility policy are declined. Eligible applications go to Risk Review. An unresolved material fraud concern results in decline; a cleared review proceeds to the Credit Approver.

## Approval and offer
The Credit Approver records approve or decline with a reason. Approved applications receive an offer. Rejected or expired offers close the application. Accepted offers proceed to agreement signing and consent verification. Missing signatures or consents are requested.

## Disbursement
The Disbursement Team validates beneficiary account details and initiates transfer. Successful transfer marks the loan disbursed. Failed transfers are investigated, corrected and retried or escalated.
